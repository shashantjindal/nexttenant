Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

This explains what NextTenant collects, why, who can see it, and how to get it changed or deleted. It describes what the site actually does — not what a privacy policy usually says.

The short version

Your phone number and address are never shown on a public page. They are sent to one other person only when you accept their request, and you can take that access away afterwards. We do not sell anything to anyone, we run no advertising, and there are no third-party trackers on this site.

Who is responsible

NextTenant operates this site and decides how the personal data described below is handled. In the language of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data Principal.

What we collect, and why

To let you sign in

  • Your email address. It is how you sign in — there are no passwords on this site. We send a six-digit code to it each time.
  • Your mobile number. Collected when you first sign up, so that somebody you connect with can reach you on WhatsApp. It is not shown on any public page.
  • The codes themselves are never stored. We keep a one-way cryptographic hash, check your code against it, and delete the record once it expires.

To show you on a listing

  • Your first name, and optionally your gender and age bracket. These appear publicly, on listings you create and on requests you send. We deliberately never ask for a surname.
  • A work email, if you choose to verify one. Only the FACT that it is verified is ever shown. The address itself is not published.

If you list a room

  • Details of the flat — area, size, rent, deposit, photos, move-in date. These are public; that is the point of a listing.
  • Its location, kept apart from the listing. The public page shows an approximate circle a few hundred metres from the real position. The exact point, the building or society name, a full address and a Maps link if you add one are stored separately and are not published.
  • Photos are re-encoded the moment they are uploaded. Phones write the location where a picture was taken inside the image file. We strip that and never store the original bytes — without this, the exact address of your flat would be readable from your own photographs.

While you use the site

  • Rooms you save, requests you send and receive, and searches you save.
  • A count of how many times a listing has been viewed — a number per listing, not a record of who looked.
  • A session, so you stay signed in. See Cookies below.
  • Reports and moderation decisions, if you report a listing or if we act on one. We record what was decided and by whom, because an account can be removed this way and that has to remain answerable.

Who can see your contact details

This is the part that matters most, so it is stated exactly.

  • Your phone number, the exact location of your flat, its society name and its full address are never on a public page and are not visible to other users by default.
  • They are released to one specific person at one specific moment: when you accept the request they sent you about a room. At that point you can see their number and they can see yours.
  • You can remove that access afterwards from your requests page. We cannot, of course, make them forget a number they have already seen — nobody can — but it stops being available through this site.

Who else processes it

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising. A small number of services handle it on our behalf so the site can run:

  • Amazon Web Services — hosting, the database and photo storage. Our servers and database are in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region, so this data stays in India.
  • Amazon Simple Email Service — sends your sign-in codes and notification emails.
  • Cloudflare — routes traffic to the site and therefore handles the connection, including your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles are fetched by your browser directly from their servers, so your IP address reaches them when a map is on screen. The approximate circle is all that is ever plotted; the exact point is never sent to a map provider.

Cookies

One cookie, named SESSION. It keeps you signed in and contains nothing but a random identifier — the session itself lives in our database, not in your browser. It lasts up to 30 days, and signing out deletes it on both sides.

There are no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no third-party trackers on this site, which is why you are not being asked to accept any.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in codes — deleted shortly after they expire.
  • Listings — until you take them down; they stop being public 30 days after publication unless you extend them.
  • Your account and its contact details — until you ask us to delete them.
  • Moderation records — kept after an account is removed, because a record of a decision that can be erased by the person it concerns is not a record.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you, and who it has been shared with.
  • Correct anything inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Most of it you can edit yourself from your dashboard.
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw consent — which, since an account here exists to connect you with somebody, means deleting the account.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.

Write to the address below. We will ask you to confirm the request from the email address on the account, because otherwise anyone could ask us to delete somebody else's.

Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Contact details are stored apart from public data, sign-in codes are stored only as hashes, photo location data is destroyed on upload, and sessions can be ended server-side. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you and the Data Protection Board if a breach affects your personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top and, for anything significant, tell you by email.
